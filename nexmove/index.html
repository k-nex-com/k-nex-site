FAQ

Does NexMove delete anything from my Dropbox?

No. NexMove only reads your Dropbox. It never changes, moves or deletes your Dropbox files. You decide when to cancel.

Can K-NEX see my files?

No. NexMove runs on your own Windows PC. Your files go from Dropbox to your computer and on to Google Drive. There are no servers in between, not ours and not anyone else’s. See our Privacy Policy.

Do I need free space on my computer?

No. Unlike copying between the Dropbox and Google Drive desktop apps, NexMove never saves your files to your hard drive. You only need room to install the app.

What can NexMove see in my Google Drive?

Only the files it creates. NexMove copies everything into a folder called “From Dropbox” and cannot see or change any of your other Google Drive files.

What if my internet drops halfway?

Open NexMove again and press Start. It checks what is already in Google Drive and copies only the files that are still missing. Files already copied are not copied again.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

Yes, while it copies. Your files travel through your own PC instead of someone else’s server, which is what keeps them private. You can keep using your computer, or leave it on overnight. If it goes to sleep, shuts down or loses internet, nothing is lost: open NexMove again and it copies only the files that are left.
