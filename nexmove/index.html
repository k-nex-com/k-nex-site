/ HOW IT WORKS

FAQ

Which moves does NexMove do, and which not?

Three, in one app. You pick one when you open it:

  • Dropbox → Google Drive
  • Dropbox → OneDrive
  • OneDrive → Google Drive

It does not do:

  • Moves out of Google Drive, such as Google Drive → Dropbox or Google Drive → OneDrive.
  • Moves into Dropbox, such as OneDrive → Dropbox.
  • Moves within one service, such as one Dropbox or OneDrive account to another.
  • Work or school OneDrive (OneDrive for Business) or SharePoint. Only personal Microsoft accounts.
  • Files in OneDrive Personal Vault. Move them out of the vault first.
  • Other services, such as iCloud Drive, Box or Mega.
Does NexMove delete anything where my files are now?

No. NexMove only copies from your Dropbox or OneDrive. It never changes, moves or deletes anything there. You decide when to cancel.

Can K-NEX see my files?

No. NexMove runs on your own Windows PC. Your files go from Dropbox or OneDrive to your computer and on to Google Drive or OneDrive. There are no servers in between, not ours and not anyone else’s. See our Privacy Policy.

Do I need free space on my computer?

No. Unlike copying between two cloud desktop apps, NexMove never saves your files to your hard drive. You only need room to install the app.

What can NexMove see where my files go?

In Google Drive, only the files it creates: it cannot see or change any of your other Google Drive files. OneDrive has no permission that narrow, so NexMove asks for access to your OneDrive files, but it only adds its own folder and does not change or delete anything else. Your files go into a new folder called “From Dropbox” or “From OneDrive”.

What if my internet drops halfway?

Open NexMove again and press Start. It checks what has already arrived and copies only the files that are still missing. Files already copied are not copied again.

I have two accounts. Do I need two licences?

Yes. One licence moves one account you move from, a Dropbox or a OneDrive. Where the files go does not matter.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

Yes, while it copies. Your files travel through your own PC instead of someone else’s server, which is what keeps them private. You can keep using your computer, or leave it on overnight. If it goes to sleep, shuts down or loses internet, nothing is lost: open NexMove again and it copies only the files that are left.
