/ HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. Nothing to set up.

  1. 01 · PICK YOUR MOVE

    Where your files are, and where they go

    Dropbox to Google Drive, Dropbox to OneDrive, or OneDrive to Google Drive.

  2. 02 · SIGN IN TO BOTH

    On their own pages

    You sign in on Dropbox’s, Microsoft’s and Google’s own pages, so NexMove never sees your passwords. It checks there is enough room before anything starts.

  3. 03 · PRESS START

    Copied, then checked

    Your files go into a new folder, then NexMove checks that each one arrived at the same size as the original. If it stops, it copies only what is left next time.

Your files travel from your old cloud, through your own PC, into the new one. No server sits in between — not ours, not anyone’s — and nothing is saved to your hard drive.

FAQ

Who is behind K-NEX?+

Tim Kim, a sole trader in the United Kingdom. K-NEX is one person, so when you email, the person who builds NexMove reads it and replies.

Can K-NEX see my files?+

No. The app runs only on your computer. Your files go from your Dropbox or OneDrive, through your PC, to your Google Drive or OneDrive, never through our servers or anyone else’s.

Which moves does NexMove do, and which not?+

Three, in one app. You pick one when you open it:

  • Dropbox → Google Drive
  • Dropbox → OneDrive
  • OneDrive → Google Drive

It does not do:

  • Moves out of Google Drive, such as Google Drive → Dropbox or Google Drive → OneDrive.
  • Moves into Dropbox, such as OneDrive → Dropbox.
  • Moves within one service, such as one Dropbox or OneDrive account to another.
  • Work or school OneDrive (OneDrive for Business) or SharePoint. Only personal Microsoft accounts.
  • Files in OneDrive Personal Vault. Move them out of the vault first.
  • Other services, such as iCloud Drive, Box or Mega.
Do I need to be good with computers?+

Not at all. Pick your move, sign in to both accounts and press Start. There are no settings to understand. If you get stuck, the person who built it will help you directly.
