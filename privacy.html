NexMove (desktop app)

NexMove copies your files from Dropbox to Google Drive. It runs entirely on your own Windows computer. Your files travel from Dropbox to your computer and on to Google Drive; they never pass through, and are never stored on, K-NEX servers.

Signing in to Dropbox and Google

You sign in on Dropbox’s and Google’s own web pages. K-NEX never sees your passwords. After you sign in, Dropbox and Google give NexMove an access token, which is saved only on your computer, in NexMove’s settings folder. It is never sent to K-NEX.

  • Dropbox: NexMove reads your files and folders so it can copy them. It does not change, move or delete anything in your Dropbox.
  • Google Drive: NexMove asks only for access to files it creates itself (the drive.file permission). It creates a folder called “From Dropbox”, copies your files into it and checks them afterwards. It cannot see or change any other files in your Google Drive.

What stays on your computer

NexMove keeps a log of what it copied and any errors, and remembers which folders you chose, so it can carry on where it stopped. These stay on your computer. NexMove has no analytics or tracking and does not send usage data to K-NEX.

When something is sent to K-NEX

Only when you press “Send report” or “Get help”. Before anything is sent, NexMove shows you exactly what will be sent. Sign-in tokens are always removed, you can choose to hide file and folder names, and the contents of your files are never sent. See the table above for what a report contains and how long we keep it.

Google user data

NexMove’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. NexMove uses Google Drive access only to copy your files into your own Google Drive and to check that they arrived. That data stays on your computer and in your Google Drive. It is not transferred to K-NEX or anyone else, not used for advertising, and not read by people.

Removing NexMove’s access

Uninstalling NexMove deletes the app, but you can also remove its access at any time:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Dropbox: dropbox.com/account/connected_apps

Files NexMove copied stay in your Google Drive until you delete them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, we will tell early-access members by email.
