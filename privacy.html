Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED · 30 OCTOBER 2026

This policy explains what personal data K-NEX collects through this website, why, and what rights you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

K-NEX is run by Tim Kim in the United Kingdom. Tim Kim is the data controller for personal data collected through k-nex.com. You can reach us at tim.kim@k-nex.com.

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. We do not sell your data or use it for profiling.

Who processes your data for us

  • Formspree receives the forms on this site and passes them to us.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) serves the typefaces this site uses.
  • Our email provider stores the messages we receive and send.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where your data is transferred outside the UK, we rely on the safeguards permitted by UK data protection law, such as the UK–US data bridge or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Early access emails: until you unsubscribe, or 12 months after the product you signed up for launches, whichever comes first.
  • Messages: up to 24 months after our last exchange, then deleted.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • correct it if it is wrong
  • delete it
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • send it to you or another organisation in a portable format

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time by emailing us or using the unsubscribe link in any email we send. To exercise any of these rights, email tim.kim@k-nex.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to resolve it with you first.

Our products

Our apps are designed to run on your own computer. Files you move with them go directly between your cloud accounts and do not pass through K-NEX servers. Each product will come with its own privacy notice at launch.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, we will tell early-access members by email.
